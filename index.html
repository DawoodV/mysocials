Dawood

My name is Dawood, and I shoot photos on old vintage digital cameras.

There's something about the way they render color and light that modern cameras just don't. Modern cameras aim for precision. These old cameras don't. They produce images with imperfections caused by their limitations. What they couldn't do is exactly what gives them their character.

They remind me of memories. Memories were never meant to be perfect. They're meant to preserve how something felt, not just how it looked.

Somehow, I feel those old cameras got that right.
